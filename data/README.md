# Master Reference Catalogue: Books Shelf

This master reference catalogue documents the curated reading collection presented on the Personal shelf (`personal.qmd`), divided into four distinct sub-shelves. For each title, this catalogue records full bibliographic details, edition info, cover art provenance, and curator annotations.

---

## 1. Evolutionary Biology

Foundational texts on natural selection, gene-centric evolutionary biology, extended phenotypic expression, and evolutionary psychology.

### 1.1 The Gene: An Intimate History
- **Author**: Siddhartha Mukherjee
- **Citation**: Mukherjee, S. (2016). *The Gene: An Intimate History*. New York: Scribner. ISBN: 978-1476733500.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-the-gene.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9781476733500-L.jpg`), Scribner / Simon & Schuster.
- **Curator Note**: A panoramic history of genetics from Mendel to modern gene editing and human heredity.

### 1.2 The Selfish Gene
- **Author**: Richard Dawkins
- **Citation**: Dawkins, R. (1976; 40th anniversary ed. 2016). *The Selfish Gene*. Oxford: Oxford University Press. ISBN: 978-0198788607.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-the-selfish-gene.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9780198788607-L.jpg`), Oxford University Press.
- **Curator Note**: The foundational work articulating the gene-centric view of evolution and replicator dynamics.

### 1.3 The Extended Phenotype: The Long Reach of the Gene
- **Author**: Richard Dawkins
- **Citation**: Dawkins, R. (1982; revised ed. 2016). *The Extended Phenotype: The Long Reach of the Gene*. Oxford: Oxford University Press. ISBN: 978-0198788911.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-the-extended-phenotype.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9780198788911-L.jpg`), Oxford University Press.
- **Curator Note**: Dawkins's masterwork demonstrating that phenotypic gene expression extends outward into the environment.

### 1.4 The Red Queen: Sex and the Evolution of Human Nature
- **Author**: Matt Ridley
- **Citation**: Ridley, M. (1993; reprint ed. 2003). *The Red Queen: Sex and the Evolution of Human Nature*. New York: Harper Perennial. ISBN: 978-0060556570.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-the-red-queen.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9780060556570-L.jpg`), HarperCollins.
- **Curator Note**: An investigation into sexual reproduction and the co-evolutionary arms race between species.

### 1.5 The Mating Mind: How Sexual Choice Shaped the Evolution of Human Nature
- **Author**: Geoffrey Miller
- **Citation**: Miller, G. (2000). *The Mating Mind: How Sexual Choice Shaped the Evolution of Human Nature*. New York: Doubleday / Anchor Books. ISBN: 978-0385495172.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-the-mating-mind.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9780385495172-L.jpg`), Anchor Books.
- **Curator Note**: A thesis proposing human intelligence, humor, and art evolved through sexual selection as courtship adaptations.

## 2. General Science & Mathematics

Influential works on cognitive heuristics, mathematical intuition, nonlinear dynamics, complexity, and scientific inquiry.

### 2.1 Thinking, Fast and Slow
- **Author**: Daniel Kahneman
- **Citation**: Kahneman, D. (2011). *Thinking, Fast and Slow*. New York: Farrar, Straus and Giroux. ISBN: 978-0374275631.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-thinking-fast-and-slow.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9780374275631-L.jpg`), Farrar, Straus and Giroux.
- **Curator Note**: A landmark distillation of dual-process psychology, heuristics, and systematic cognitive biases.

### 2.2 The Joy of X: A Guided Tour of Math, from One to Infinity
- **Author**: Steven Strogatz
- **Citation**: Strogatz, S. (2012). *The Joy of X: A Guided Tour of Math, from One to Infinity*. Boston: Eamon Dolan / Houghton Mifflin Harcourt. ISBN: 978-0547517650.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-the-joy-of-x.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9780547517650-L.jpg`), Houghton Mifflin Harcourt.
- **Curator Note**: A warm, intuitive guided tour through the core ideas and universal beauty of mathematics.

### 2.3 Alex's Adventures in Numberland
- **Author**: Alex Bellos
- **Citation**: Bellos, A. (2010). *Alex's Adventures in Numberland*. London: Bloomsbury Publishing. ISBN: 978-1408809594.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-alexs-adventures-in-numberland.jpg`

### 2.4 The Pattern Seekers: How Autism Drives Human Invention
- **Author**: Simon Baron-Cohen
- **Citation**: Baron-Cohen, S. (2020). *The Pattern Seekers: How Autism Drives Human Invention*. New York: Basic Books. ISBN: 978-1541647145.

### 2.5 Chaos: Making a New Science
- **Author**: James Gleick
- **Citation**: Gleick, J. (1987; 20th anniversary ed. 2008). *Chaos: Making a New Science*. New York: Penguin Books. ISBN: 978-0143113454.

### 2.6 Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!: Adventures of a Curious Character

## 4. Textbooks

Authoritative references in information theory, dynamical systems, and rigorous empirical statistics.

### 4.1 Information Theory, Inference and Learning Algorithms
- **Author**: David J. C. MacKay
- **Citation**: MacKay, D. J. C. (2003). *Information Theory, Inference and Learning Algorithms*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. ISBN: 978-0521642989.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-information-theory-inference-and-learning-algorithms.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Cambridge University Press / `https://www.inference.org.uk/itprnn/book.html` / Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9780521642989-L.jpg`).
- **Curator Note**: A tour de force unifying Shannon information theory, Bayesian inference, and statistical machine learning.

### 4.2 Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos: With Applications to Physics, Biology, Chemistry, and Engineering
- **Author**: Steven H. Strogatz
- **Citation**: Strogatz, S. H. (1994; 2nd ed. 2014). *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos: With Applications to Physics, Biology, Chemistry, and Engineering*. Boulder, CO: Westview Press / CRC Press. ISBN: 978-0813349107.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-nonlinear-dynamics-and-chaos.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9780813349107-L.jpg`), CRC Press.
- **Curator Note**: The definitive, visually intuitive introduction to differential equations, bifurcations, and phase space topology.

### 4.3 Statistics
- **Author**: David Freedman, Robert Pisani, & Roger Purves
- **Citation**: Freedman, D., Pisani, R., & Purves, R. (1978; 4th ed. 2007). *Statistics* (4th ed.). New York: W. W. Norton & Company. ISBN: 978-0393929720.
- **Cover Image**: `files/images/shelf/book-statistics-freedman.jpg`
- **Image Provenance**: Open Library Covers API (`https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/9780393929720-L.jpg`), W. W. Norton & Company.
- **Curator Note**: An uncompromisingly conceptual, non-formulaic masterclass in statistical reasoning, observational studies, and experimental controls.
